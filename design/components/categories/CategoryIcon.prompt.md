# CategoryIcon
A category's drawn glyph on a solid square in its own color — used in day panels, entry cards and category lists.

```jsx
<CategoryIcon icon="plane" color="blue" />
<CategoryIcon icon="plane" color="blue" filled={false} />  {/* softer glyph-on-tint */}
```

Glyphs come from the drawn icon family; emoji are banned as category icons.
